PMHNP Guiding Principles

Professional Philosophy

Combining more than eight years of critical care experience with compassionate, evidence-based psychiatric-mental health practice.

Philosophy of Psychiatric-Mental Health Care

My nursing philosophy is based on the belief that mental health and physical health are closely connected. After more than eight years of caring for critically ill patients in the Intensive Care Unit and working as a Vascular Access Consultant, I have seen how medical stress, complex diagnoses, and major life events can affect a person’s mental well-being.

As a Psychiatric-Mental Health Nurse Practitioner student, I aim to provide complete, trauma-informed, and evidence-based care that respects each person’s dignity and life experiences. Whether I am completing psychiatric evaluations for adolescents, adults, or older adults, working on medication plans, or supporting treatments such as Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS), my goal is to help patients and families through clear teaching, strong therapeutic relationships, and care plans that fit their individual needs.

Thorough Clinical Assessment

Using the careful assessment skills I developed in critical care to check for medical causes of symptoms and safely manage patients who have both medical and psychiatric needs.

Evidence-Based Care & TMS

Combining proven medications and therapies with treatments like TMS mapping to support people with mood disorders that have not responded well to other approaches.

Patient Education & Shared Decisions

Explaining clinical information in clear, everyday language so patients and their families feel informed, respected, and involved in every part of their care plan.

Team Collaboration

Working closely with psychiatrists, primary care providers, therapists, nurses, and community resources to keep care connected across outpatient and inpatient settings.
